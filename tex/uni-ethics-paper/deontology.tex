Rawls uses his famous \emph{veil of ignorance} to argue that rational agents agree on principles of justice that ensure the best outcome for those that are currently in the worst position. Intuitively, if one cannot tell whether they will end up in poor or good health, then they would rationally only favour inequalities which improve everyone's situation. Statistical profiling fails this test, as it requires those in poor situations to pay more, thus sending them a downwards spiral of degrading circumstances, since wealth and health are strongly postively correlated~\cite{health-socdec}. Indeed, under Rawls's contractualism, one should then side against profiling, since they might end up on the poor end of the spectrum once the veil is lifted up.

Scanlon argued that moral principles are those that cannot reasonably be rejected: ``In order for a principle to be reasonably rejectable there must be some relevant standpoint from which people typically have good reason''~\cite[Page~218]{what-we-owe}. Of course, those in poor conditions can reasonably reject the principle of allowing health insurance companies to perform statistical profiling, by bringing up arguments like the fact it puts a larger burden on those who already find themselves in terrible situations. On the other hand, healthy people woudln't be able to reasonably make the converse argument for supporting statistical profiling, since they do not find themselves in the same terrible situations.

Of course, our analysis so far has been quite rough, as the arguments' effectiveness varies greatly based on the specific risk factor being used for profiling. For one, an unhealthy person would have a hard time reasonably rejecting statistical profiling if they themselves are responsible for their poor health condition, thus making profiling permissible under Scanlon's theory. One could for example similarly argue under Rawls's theory that a person who smokes could reasonably agree even when under the veil of ignorance that they deserve to pay more for their insurance, regardless of whether they are in fact sick once the veil is lifted up.
